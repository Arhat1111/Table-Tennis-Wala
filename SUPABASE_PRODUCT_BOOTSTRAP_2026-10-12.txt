TTW Supabase product bootstrap fix

Why ttw_products was empty:
The built-in catalogue lived in script.js and only admin-created products were previously written to ttw_products.

New behavior:
1. Sign in to /admin.html with the registered Supabase admin account.
2. If ttw_products is empty, the current active built-in catalogue is uploaded automatically in batches.
3. The admin panel also includes an “Initialize catalogue in Supabase” button for a manual retry.
4. Supabase product rows override built-in records only by exact product ID, preventing name/image mismatches.
